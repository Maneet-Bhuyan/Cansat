    - Dataset: 27,000 satellite/aerial image tiles (64x64 RGB, 10 spectral classes).
    - Mapped EuroSAT 10 spectral classes into 4 CanSat Safe Landing Area Index (SLAI) tiers:
      * Tier 0: SAFE_LZ (Pasture, Herbaceous Vegetation)
      * Tier 1: OBSTACLE_CANOPY (Forest canopy, dense trees)
