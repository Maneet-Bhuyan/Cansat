Engineering Roadmap

Current Limitations & Future Scope

Honest boundary conditions identified during hardware flight tests, and the architectural upgrades planned for the next mission cycle.

Current Operational Limitations

Boundary Conditions

1. Low-Light & Twilight Descent

Under illuminance below 50 lux, the small CMOS sensor aperture suffers high signal-to-noise ratio degradation, diminishing hazard edge detection accuracy.

2. High Angular Tumble Rates (>150 deg/s)

During parachute deployment turbulence, rotational velocities exceeding 150 deg/s cause rolling-shutter motion blur on the OV3660 camera.

3. Fixed Focal Length

The wide-angle lens is hyper-focused at infinity for high altitudes, causing minor optical defocusing in the final 3 meters prior to touchdown.

Future Mission Scope

Next Generation

1. Steerable Ram-Air Parafoil Actuation

Integrating micro-servo winches to pull steering lines on a ram-air parafoil, translating the commanded EVADE theta vector into active glide path corrections.

2. Optical Flow Odometry

Computing frame-to-frame pixel disparity directly on the ESP32 to measure wind-induced horizontal ground drift even during GPS denial.

3. Multi-Spectral Micro-Sensors

Augmenting the RGB optical array with an 8x8 thermopile infrared sensor to detect bodies of water and thermal hazards through smoke and dust.
