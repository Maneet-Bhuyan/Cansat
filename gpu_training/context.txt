COGNITIVE CANSAT - ARCHITECTURAL CONTEXT & SAFE LANDING AI (SLAI) SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
-------------------
The Cognitive CanSat system is an end-to-end sounding pico-satellite telemetry,
flight prognostics, and real-time computer vision platform. During the terminal
parachute descent phase (typically descending at 4 to 6 m/s), an optical nadir camera
samples the approaching ground to determine whether the touchdown zone is safe
or if lateral emergency steering / ejection is required.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT & DIAGNOSIS
--------------------------------
The initial airborne TinyML model (TinyLandingNet, 22k params) exhibited false-positive
safe landing classifications when shown non-aerial scenes, such as human faces or
indoor rooms.

Root cause diagnosis:
1. Closed-set Softmax Limitation: Standard classifiers assign probability 1.0 across
   their known training classes. When presented with completely out-of-distribution (OOD)
   inputs (e.g. human face, indoor drywall, furniture), the network is forced to pick one
   of the 4 flight terrain classes.
2. In-browser Color Heuristic: Previous prototype JavaScript routines evaluated a
   simplified VARI vegetation index (G-R)/(G+R-B), which under certain indoor warm lighting
   can mimic low-vegetation ground reflection.
3. Model Capacity: While TinyLandingNet is small enough to fit inside 142 KB SRAM on an
   ESP32-CAM, real-world deployment requires complementary heavy-duty models for edge
   companion computers and ground stations to verify terrain textures and obstacles.

3. ARCHITECTURAL RESOLUTION: 3-TIER VISION & OOD GUARD
------------------------------------------------------
To resolve this, the vision system is divided into two distinct components:

Component A: Out-of-Distribution (OOD) Aerial Safety Guard (ml/vision_guard.py)
- Implemented in Python and mirrored in JavaScript (frontend/dashboard.html and
  frontend/js/tinyml-demo.js).
- Audits incoming optical frames before any hazard classification:
  a. Peer/Kovac skin chromaticity test (R > 95, G > 40, B > 20, |R-G| > 15, R > G, R > B).
     If skin ratio > 14%, immediate rejection as HUMAN_SUBJECT_DETECTED.
  b. Flat-field indoor illumination & texture variance test. Detects uniform drywall,
     painted ceilings, and desaturated indoor lighting lacking aerial multispectral variance.
  c. Shannon Entropy check across model softmax distribution. High entropy (> 0.88)
     flags severe classification uncertainty.
- When OOD is triggered:
  - slai_class is set to INVALID_AERIAL_SURFACE.
  - Optimal LZ bounding boxes and evasion vectors are suppressed.
  - HUD displays: "SLAI: OOD_REJECTED (NON-AERIAL SCENE)".

Component B: 3-Tier Multi-Scale Vision Hierarchy
- Tier 1: TinyLandingNet (22,548 params, ~88 KB FP32 / 23 KB INT8)
  Target: Airborne ESP32-S3 / ESP32-CAM flight microcontroller.
  Operational Role: Sub-5ms nadir hazard gating during airborne freefall.
- Tier 2: MobileNetV3-Small (1,521,956 params, ~5.8 MB FP32 / 1.48 MB INT8)
  Target: Companion computer (Raspberry Pi 4 / CM4 / Jetson Orin Nano).
  Operational Role: High-resolution downlinked optical stream classification at 40+ FPS.
- Tier 3: ResNet-18 (11,178,564 params, ~42.6 MB FP32 / 10.9 MB INT8)
  Target: Ground Station telemetry workstation / Cloud Lakehouse.
  Operational Role: Deep residual verification, orthomosaic mapping, and post-flight review.

4. DATASET & CLASS HIERARCHY
----------------------------
Dataset: EuroSAT 2750 Multispectral RGB satellite imagery (27,000 images, 64x64 native).
Stored at: data/eurosat/2750/

The 10 EuroSAT classes map into 4 Safe Landing Area Index (SLAI) tiers:
- SLAI Class 0: SAFE_LZ
  Includes: AnnualCrop, HerbaceousVegetation, Pasture, PermanentCrop.
  Characteristics: Flat, soft, non-damaging landing surface with clear touchdown line-of-sight.
- SLAI Class 1: OBSTACLE_CANOPY
  Includes: Forest.
  Characteristics: Tree crowns, canopy snag hazards. Triggers advisory lateral evasion.
- SLAI Class 2: CRITICAL_HAZARD
  Includes: Highway, Industrial, Residential.
  Characteristics: Asphalt, moving vehicles, structures, power lines. Triggers emergency evasion.
- SLAI Class 3: WATER_HAZARD
  Includes: River, SeaLake.
  Characteristics: Open water surfaces. Immediate drowning hazard for electronics.
