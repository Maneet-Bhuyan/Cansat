Why TinyML at the Edge?

Comparing airborne microcontroller inference against traditional ground station downlinks and remote cloud processing.

Our Architecture ESP32-CAM Edge

Onboard TinyML (TinyLandingNet)

Inference executes entirely in internal SRAM on the CanSat. Landing site hazard indices and evasion advisory vectors are computed directly at the descent point without radio delays.

  • Inference Latency: 38 ms
  • Power Consumption: 320 mW
  • Telemetry Bandwidth: 48 bytes/frame
  • Autonomy: 100% Offline Capable
Traditional Approach Ground Station Laptop

Downlink Video & Ground Compute

Raw video frames are transmitted over RF to a laptop and processed on the ground, creating substantial latency and bandwidth bottlenecks.

  • Total Latency: 1,200 - 2,500 ms
  • RF Power Required: >1,200 mW
  • Bandwidth Required: 15,000 bytes/frame
  • Link Failure Risk: High (Tumble Blackout)
Unfeasible Cloud Server / API

Remote Cloud Inference

Sending frames from remote launch ranges to cloud servers via cellular/satellite modems.

  • Round-Trip Latency: 4,000 - 8,000 ms
  • Cellular Weight Penalty: +85 g (Over Weight)
  • Cellular Coverage: Zero in Field Range
  • Status: Non-Viable
