Problem Formulation SLIDE 02 / 12

Three Core Failure Modes in Atmospheric Sounding Probes

Mathematical formulation of sensor corruption, aerodynamic oscillation, and open-set vision faults

1. Barometric Venturi Depression

  • Physical Mechanism: Airflow past cylindrical vent ports creates Bernoulli suction dP_q = -0.5 * c_p * rho * vz^2.
  • Positive Altitude Bias: Induces systematic offset dh_q = +c_p * vz^2 / (2g) * (1 + 0.15 sin^2(psi)) during rapid freefall and canopy descent.
  • Why Linear Filters Fail: Causal Savitzky-Golay smoothing (1.778 m RMSE) smooths white noise directly around the biased trajectory without removing the Venturi offset.

2. Pendulum Countdown Divergence

  • Post-Burst Dynamics: Pyrotechnic deployment (>5G shock) excites coupled parachute pendulum oscillation (+/-18 deg pitch/roll).
  • Velocity Instability: Instantaneous descent speed vz oscillates during wind shear and thermal updrafts.
  • Ballistic Breakdown: Naive division tau = h / |vz| yields 44.46 s RMSE and negative R2 = -2.01, preventing reliable terminal camera burst triggering.

3. Closed-Set Softmax Overconfidence

  • 520 KB SRAM Bottleneck: AI-Thinker ESP32-CAM cannot fit standard MobileNet or ResNet backbones in internal SRAM.
  • Open-Set Vulnerability: A 4-class softmax layer must sum to 1.0 even on Out-of-Distribution (OOD) non-aerial frames.
  • Bench-Handling Hazard: Unguarded CNNs classify human faces, indoor walls, and lens caps as SAFE_LZ with >85% confidence.
SPEAKER NOTE: Every module in our architecture directly targets one of these three physical failure modes: Venturi pressure depression, parachute pendulum velocity oscillation, and closed-set neural overconfidence.
System Architecture SLIDE 03 / 12

Dual-Tier Cyber-Physical System Architecture

Airborne dual-MCU avionics + ESP32-CAM edge vision coupled to FastAPI ML analytics and a distributed lakehouse

Tier 1: Airborne Payload & Dual RF Links (350g, 66x115mm)

  • Primary Flight MCU: Polls BMP280 (Pressure/Temp), DHT11/SHT31 (Humidity), MPU6050 6-DOF IMU (+/-16G, +/-2000 deg/s), u-blox NEO-6M GPS, and 1S LiPo ADC at 1.25 Hz (dt = 0.8 s).
  • Edge Vision Node (ESP32-CAM): Dual-core 240 MHz Xtensa LX6 captures 320x240 QVGA frames, runs TinyLandingNet INT8 (7.15 KB) across a 3x3 spatial grid, and computes Escape Vector EVADE theta deg.
  • Dual RF Downlink: 433 MHz LoRa (9600 bps CSV telemetry, 1.5 km slant range) + 2.4 GHz ESP-NOW (460,800 baud UART video bridge).

Tier 2: Ground Station Analytics & Lakehouse

  • TelemetryQualityAnalyzer: Deterministic check of packet completeness, clock jitter, GNSS (0,0) lock losses, and step anomalies before ML inference.
  • Physics & Tabular ML Suite: Hypsometric & Magnus-Tetens atmospheric engine, 1D EKF + ExtraTrees Calibrator, Random Forest Phase Classifier, PyOD Isolation Forest, and Quantile GBR Touchdown Prognostics.
  • Persistence & Streaming: SQLite WAL DB (data/cansat_missions.db, 3,898 rows) + Redpanda/Kafka + TimescaleDB + MinIO S3.
SPEAKER NOTE: By splitting hard real-time sensing and 7.15 KB INT8 hazard detection on the airborne node from ensemble ML and lakehouse storage on the ground station, we respect the 320 mW power budget without sacrificing analytical depth.

Methodology: State Estimation SLIDE 05 / 12

Physics-Informed Hybrid EKF + Scale-Invariant ExtraTrees

Eliminating aerodynamic Venturi pressure depression without train-test leakage or altitude extrapolation clipping

Stage 1: Adaptive 1D Constant-Velocity EKF

  • State Vector: x_k = [h_k, vz_k]^T driven by vertical inertial acceleration u_k = (az_k - 1.0) * g0.
  • Non-Uniform Timing Support: Transition matrices F_k and process covariance Q_k adapt per packet to handle LoRa UART jitter (8 ms to 199 ms).
  • High-G Pyrotechnic Gate: When |a_k| > 2.5G during apogee separation, process variance sigma_q^2 scales 10x to prevent filter lag.

Stage 2: Scale-Invariant ExtraTrees Residual Calibrator

  • Why Direct Altitude Trees Clip: Tree ensembles predicting absolute altitude cannot extrapolate when test apogees (up to 1,250 m) exceed training altitudes.
  • Scale-Invariant Residual Learning: Our residual_pipeline predicts the aerodynamic bias b_hat_k = f_ET(u_k) from 13 aerodynamic features (dynamic pressure proxy, tilt sin^2(psi), |a|, |omega|).
  • Disjoint Training Set: Trained on 80 independent flights (seed=1001, 35,342 samples) with unknown Venturi coefficients c_p in [0.38, 0.62].
  • Convex Fusion: h_hat_k = 0.25 * h_EKF + 0.75 * (h_raw - b_hat_k).
SPEAKER NOTE: Predicting scale-invariant aerodynamic residuals rather than absolute altitude allows our ExtraTrees compensator to generalize across any launch elevation or apogee ceiling without tree extrapolation clipping.

Open-Set Vision Safety SLIDE 09 / 12

Five-Stage Out-of-Distribution (OOD) AerialVisionGuard

100% rejection of non-aerial inputs (human faces, indoor walls, lens caps, blur) in <1.8 ms overhead

Pre-Neural Statistical & Spectral Gates (Stages 1-3)

  • Stage 1 (Luminance & Saturation Gate): Rejects lens-cap darkness (mu_Y < 18), overexposure (mu_Y > 245), or uniform occlusion (sigma_Y < 6.0).
  • Stage 2 (YCbCr Skin-Tone & VARI Portrait Gate): Transforms RGB to YCbCr (Cb in [77,127], Cr in [133,173]). Rejects close-up human faces when central skin ratio > 0.22, R > G + 12, and vegetation index VARI < -0.04.
  • Stage 3 (Laplacian Micro-Texture Gate): Rejects flat indoor walls and severe blur when Var(laplacian(Y)) < 14.0.

Post-Neural Energy & Physical Cross-Checks (Stages 4-5)

  • Stage 4 (Helmholtz Free Energy & Shannon Entropy): Computes E(x;T) = -T ln sum exp(z_c/T) and normalized entropy H(p). Rejects frames with H(p) > 0.78 or max_c p_c < 0.48.
  • Stage 5 (VARI Spectral-Neural Cross-Check): Overrides false >70% SAFE_LZ or OBSTACLE_CANOPY predictions if physical vegetation index VARI < -0.06.
  • Benchmark Result: 100% rejection on all 4 OOD categories across all 3 CNN tiers while passing 100% of real drone descent videos.
SPEAKER NOTE: During live webcam testing, pointing an unguarded satellite CNN at a human face produces a false SAFE_LZ clearance. Our 5-stage AerialVisionGuard catches 100% of portraits, occlusions, and indoor walls in under 1.8 ms.
